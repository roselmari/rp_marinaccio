To Set Up Each Trial

1

Take six 50mL tubes and add 10mL algae solution, 15-25 duckweed fronds, and/or 5mL of glyphosate solution according to the diagram.

2

Leave tubes under a grow light for 8 hours every day for 5 days.

3

Complete first data collection immediately after making the tubes, second data collection on day 3, and third data collection on day 5.

To Collect Algae Cell Data

1

Shake the tube to disperse the algae cells, then place a drop on the hemocytometer with a pipette. Cover with a cover slip.

2

Place under the microscope and adjust settings so the algae cells are clearly visible.

3

Record the number of algae cells in each corner quadrant and then find the average number between them.

4

Repeat for each of the 4 tubes containing algae cells.

To Collect Duckweed Frond Data

1

Count the number of duckweed fronds in each tube and record.

2

Take a picture of the 4 tubes next to each other and record any differences.
